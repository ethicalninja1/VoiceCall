🔒 Privacy notice

To keep the service safe, the administrator can see your name, ID, email / mobile number, IP address, device and approximate location, and whether you are online. If you allow it, your precise GPS location is shared too.

Calls, video and in-call chat are private (peer-to-peer). Direct chat messages are stored on the server so they reach you when you are offline; the administrator console never shows message content. You can delete your account any time.
